The Three Doshas

Discover your prakriti

In Ayurveda, all matter is composed of five elements. These combine to form three bio-energies — or doshas — that govern every aspect of your physical and mental wellbeing.

V

Vata

Air & Space

Governs all movement in the body — breathing, circulation, nerve impulses. When in balance: creative, lively, enthusiastic.

Creative Energetic Quick mind Slender
P

Pitta

Fire & Water

Governs transformation and metabolism — digestion, intelligence, ambition. When balanced: sharp, focused and courageous.

Focused Ambitious Sharp wit Athletic
K

Kapha

Earth & Water

Governs structure and lubrication — strength, stability, love and compassion. When balanced: calm, patient and nurturing.

Calm Grounded Strong Nurturing
